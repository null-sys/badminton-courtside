How ratings work

Everyone starts at 1000. Beating a stronger side earns more than beating a weaker one, and a big win moves the rating more than a close one (21–10 counts about 40% more than 21–19). Deuce games (22–20, 25–23, 30–29…) count as the closest possible result. A game that didn't finish counts as a draw (half a win): it only moves points from the higher-rated side to the lower-rated one, and win % counts it as half. In doubles both partners gain or lose the same amount. “Week” is the change since Monday.
